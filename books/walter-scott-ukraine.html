Books

Shliakh sera Val’tera Skotta na Ukrainu

English title

The Path of Sir Walter Scott to Ukraine: The Adaptation of the Historical Novel Genre of Walter Scott by N. Gogol in Taras Bulba and by P. Kulish in The Black Council

Ukrainian title

Шлях сера Вальтера Скотта на Україну («Тарас Бульба» М. Гоголя і «Чорна рада» П. Куліша в світлі історичної романістики Вальтера Скотта)

Description

A comparative study of how the historical novel of Sir Walter Scott shaped two founding narratives of the Cossack past: Nikolai Gogol’s Taras Bulba and Panteleimon Kulish’s The Black Council (Chorna rada). Written in English and published in Ukrainian translation by the Kyiv journal Vsesvit, the book develops the subject of the author’s doctoral dissertation at the University of Toronto.

Details

  • Kyiv: Vsesvit, 1993. 296 pages.
  • In Ukrainian. Translated from English by Ludmila Sharinova.
  • ISBN 5-7372-0016-7
